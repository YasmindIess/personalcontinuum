# I / YOU / THEY — Perspective Operators

`I`, `YOU`, and `THEY` are not properties of a person and never authorize Blochfield to speak in a cited person's voice. They are perspective operators on a situated relation.

## I — backcenter / reflexive

The projection is explicitly anchored in the curator/founder-side continuum: what this public signal changes, exposes, or makes available from the backcenter's situated perspective.

## YOU — addressed / invitational

The projection is directed toward the cited origin as an invitation to inspect, correct, extend or decline association. It must not state private motives or imply endorsement.

## THEY — observational / public

The projection stays in third-person public observation: what cited material supports and what Blochfield interprets from it without impersonating either origin.

## V2.1 compatibility

V2.1 assigned stance as a rank-based `I -> YOU -> THEY` cycle. The imported manifest preserves that assignment with `voiceStanceStatus: legacy-seeded` so history is reproducible. Production Relation Seeds should set stance deliberately and record whether it was `curated` or `relation-derived`.
