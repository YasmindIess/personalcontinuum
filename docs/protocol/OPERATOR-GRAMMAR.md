# Operator Identity Grammar v0.2

The ten V2.1 operators are retained as a **basis**, not as the final count of artworks.

Every public signal receives one stable operator identity:

`OI_i = (primary, secondary, deformation, topology, corridor, seed, rendererVersion)`

## Coordinates

- **primary operator** — dominant local equation family.
- **secondary operator** — a second mathematical pressure rendered simultaneously with the primary family.
- **deformation law** — a deterministic projection transform such as torsion, shear, contraction, anisotropy or vortex drift.
- **topology motif** — the local combinatorial support: simplex fan, recursive cell, Möbius seam, braided strip, branch complex, caustic web, torus section, annular lattice, geodesic net or triangulated membrane.
- **corridor behavior** — the rule by which the provenance-bearing filament traverses the local world.
- **seed** — deterministic local variation; never evidence and never an economic metric.
- **renderer version** — reconstructibility boundary for visual identity across software changes.

## Identity rule

Two slots may share a primary operator while remaining different mathematical identities.

The renderer MUST consume all identity coordinates. A primary-family-only renderer is non-conforming because it collapses the 50 identities back into a visible ten-family cycle.

An operator identity is stable under viewport changes. Responsive rendering may reduce density or crop projection, but MUST NOT change the canonical identity tuple.

## Corridor rule

The corridor is the visual invariant. It may orbit-cross, braid, graze a boundary, hop nodes, recursively return, phase-lock, fold-through, or act as a waveguide. Its visual morphology changes while its role as continuation remains stable.

## Perspective rule

`I / YOU / THEY` is not part of the mathematical operator identity. It is a relation-view operator:

- `I` — backcenter/reflexive founder-side perspective.
- `YOU` — addressed invitation toward another situated origin.
- `THEY` — public-signal observation without impersonation.

## Public claim rule

The operator identity is an identity of the **rendered situated world**, not an identity claim about the person.

## V3 acceptance test

1. All 50 operator signatures remain unique.
2. The renderer consumes primary, secondary, deformation, topology, corridor and seed.
3. Contact sheets show strong compositional individuation while retaining one Blochfield field grammar.
4. Mobile retains name, seven-word projection, one evidence cue, one relation cue and navigation without clipping.
5. Reduced mobile density changes projection cost, not operator identity.
