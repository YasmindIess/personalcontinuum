# Charter — Personal Continuum inside Blochfield

## Object of work

Personal Continuum is the human-facing projection layer through which Blochfield can publish bounded, cited, revisable relations among public signals without collapsing a person into a profile, score, or inferred identity.

Aquarium is treated as the epistemic/operational substrate. Personal Continuum is treated as the situated rendering geometry. Their convergence is a relation-bearing public object whose history can change without its prior states being silently erased.

## Constitutional invariants

1. **Origin persistence.** A public origin remains addressable even when its local rendering is not mounted.
2. **Situated materialization.** Only the relevant neighborhood is computationally materialized.
3. **Citation before interpretation.** Public relation claims begin from addressable source material.
4. **Observation is not interpretation.** Derived meaning must remain distinguishable from literal source evidence.
5. **Interpretation is not verification.** Semantic admissibility cannot be presented as independent truth.
6. **Citation is not endorsement.** Referenced participants retain the right to dispute, extend, contextualize, or decline association.
7. **Append-only reconstitution.** Corrections and responses create new receipts/revisions; they do not rewrite historical state invisibly.
8. **No external authority by implication.** A tagged participant is not granted Blochfield execution authority; Blochfield does not claim authority over that participant.
9. **Economic projection follows evidence.** Any future claim/airdrop state is downstream of the evidence geometry, never its cause.
10. **Public ranking is curatorial.** `curatorRank` expresses founder ordering, not objective human worth or mathematically proven importance.
