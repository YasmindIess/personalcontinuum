# Think Tank — Active Research Program

Personal Continuum is developed as a product **and** as a bounded research program. Research questions are allowed to be ambitious; public claims are not allowed to outrun evidence.

## T1 — Evidential topology

**Question:** What relation features remain meaningful across heterogeneous public signals without becoming a generic similarity score?

Candidate invariants: provenance continuity, semantic overlap, structural correspondence, operator transport, bridge dependence, counterfactual removal, higher-order simplex participation.

**Falsifier:** if the same ranking can be reproduced from popularity alone, the topology is not adding the intended information.

## T2 — Operator individuation

**Question:** Can fifty artworks be recognizably distinct while remaining sections of one persistent field?

V3 now tests a stronger hypothesis: the seven-word public-signal projection itself is compiled into an ordered Semantic Heptad. Each word becomes a bounded mathematical mutation whose output state becomes the next word's input. The previous primary/secondary/deformation/topology/corridor tuple survives only as a compatibility witness.

**Falsifier:** shuffled contact sheets remain visually indistinguishable except for text.

## T3 — Situated rendering

**Question:** How little of the global field can be materialized while preserving the user's sense that the whole continues to exist?

V2.1 established a ±2 artwork window. The next experiment virtualizes textual shells, relation overlays, and expensive geometry independently.

## T4 — Mutual interpretation boundary

**Question:** Can a public system invite correction without turning citation into appropriation or participation into platform authority?

The Relation Seed protocol separates source, observation, interpretation, unresolved questions, participant response and verification.

## T5 — Attention as continuation

**Question:** Which observable actions indicate that a public artifact created meaningful continuation rather than shallow exposure?

Candidate events: source opened, relation expanded, world revisited, correction submitted, participant response attached, independently verified continuation, voluntary claim.

Raw impressions may be measured as distribution telemetry, but must not become structural-value evidence by default.

## T6 — Economic projection

**Question:** Under what conditions may structural contribution become claim/airdrop eligibility without incentives corrupting the underlying genealogy?

Economic state must remain downstream of evidence. Any allocation function must expose its inputs, version, admissibility gate, and counterfactual sensitivity.

## T7 — Aquarium homologation

**Question:** Which Personal Continuum events can be transported into Aquarium without confusing semantic admissibility, independent verification and production authority?

The target bridge is event-level: Relation Seed events become typed ingress/evidence/verdict/verification objects while public projection remains a separate authority surface.
