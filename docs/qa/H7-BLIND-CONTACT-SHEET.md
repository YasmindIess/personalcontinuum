# H7 Blind Contact Sheet

Press `A` or choose **audit 50** to open the full continuum as one blind contact sheet.

The audit intentionally hides names and seven-word phrases. Every tile shows only the slot number and a shortened H7 fingerprint. Selecting a tile returns to the normal situated-world view.

The header reports the nearest pair under the semantic separation metric. That pair is a review target, not a claim that the represented people are similar.

## Visual falsifier

The H7 individuation hypothesis fails visual QA if the contact sheet reveals a recurring family cycle that dominates the semantic composition, or if near semantic pairs collapse to effectively interchangeable silhouettes.

## Viewport contract

Desktop presents ten columns by five rows. Narrow/mobile viewports present five columns by ten rows. Both remain one-screen, no-scroll diagnostic surfaces.
